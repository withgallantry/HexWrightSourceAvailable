# Hexwright

Hex Wright is an addon for Hex Casting that brings a variety of new workstations, casting items and acessories all based around the lore of Hex Wrights. A lost faction of Hex Casters who subverted the transient nature of Hexes, etching complex lists of patterns to achieve things that aren't possible today. 

Their knowledge was considered lost with only crumbling relics to indicate they ever existed. That was until the Chronologists, a branch of Hex Casters that specialise in researching hexes of the past, pieced together a Hex they believed would activate one of the relics dotted around and cast it. It worked, but instead of just activating one relic, it activated all of them. These crumbling portals are now left to the brave to explore. Their highly unstable nature means you could either end up in a vault of a Hex Wrights most treasured artifacts, or face deep in lava hundreds of metres below the ground. You know what they say though, no risk, no reward. 

Since the initial 'Harmonic Convergence Event', as it's been called, there's been an effort to restore the lost art of the Hex Wright. Workstations and some basic items have been made public but the rest is still a mystery, either coveted information, or simply not known. Find new recipes, items and artifacts by exploring, either deep underground or by braving the unstable portals.

# Featuring
- New Crafting tree and workstations
- Customisable staffs with 80+ models to choose from
- New Hex Centric items including event driven casting items
- New weapons and armour
- New structures and events within the world
- A mix between combat and hex 'puzzles' to solve
- Progression through exploration
- New Hex Casting patterns
- New particles using [Photon](https://modrinth.com/mod/photon-editor).
- Native compatibility with JEI and EMI.
- Improved animations for flying and casting.
And much, much more!
